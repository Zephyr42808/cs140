Magic: The Gathering & Its Allure

The Fantasy

Magic: The Gathering is a table top card game that has been around for a very long time and has just regained populairty in recent years. It's a game that appeals to the basic desires of all nerds everywhere. Colossal beasts, mythical creatures, and an overarching story behind it all.


Here are some important points of Magic: The Gathering

  1. You can customize your deck how you want and have your own story
  2. Satisfy your gambling desires by searching for rare and expensive prints of cards
  3. Immerse yourself in the story of legendary heroes and villains
